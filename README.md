# Usability Testing & Analysis – Oveelo Website

## Objective

The objective of this usability testing is to evaluate the user experience of the Oveelo shopping website and identify strengths and areas for improvement.

## Website Tested

Oveelo Shopping Website

## Test Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Browse Homepage
Task: Open the homepage and explore categories.

Result: Successfully accessed categories and promotional banners.

### Scenario 2: Search Products
Task: Search for a desired product.

Result: Products were displayed clearly and navigation was easy.

### Scenario 3: View Product Details
Task: Open a product page and view information.

Result: Product images and descriptions were easy to understand.

### Scenario 4: Add Product to Cart
Task: Add an item to the shopping cart.

Result: Product was successfully added to the cart.

## Usability Findings

### Positive Aspects

- Attractive and visually appealing design
- Easy navigation through categories
- Clear product presentation
- User-friendly shopping experience

### Areas for Improvement

- Search bar visibility can be improved
- More filtering options could be added
- Loading speed can be optimized on some pages

## Conclusion

The Oveelo website provides a pleasant shopping experience with intuitive navigation and attractive visuals. Minor improvements can further enhance usability.
